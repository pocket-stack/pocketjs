# Asset and research record

## Shipped content

The example ships no third-party models, textures, animation, audio, maps, or
game data. Environment geometry and small color textures are generated at
runtime by `src/art.rs` from mathematical primitives and fixed seeds.

The explorer model, rig, materials, axe, and animation are original work
generated by `assets/character/generate_character.py`. The editable Blender
source, runtime GLB, validation receipt, and studio previews are generated from
that checked-in script. They are covered by PocketJS's MIT license.

## Research boundary

The public [zeldaret/botw](https://github.com/zeldaret/botw) project was
consulted to understand observed high-level relationships between actors,
physics contacts, physical materials, reactive materials, world conditions,
tree damage, and attached items. That repository did not provide a reuse
license at the researched revision.

No source, names, type layouts, constants, equations, configuration schemas,
shaders, extracted data, or assets from that project or from Nintendo software
are included here. Runtime types, algorithms, numeric tuning, art, controls,
and test scenarios were designed independently for PocketJS.
